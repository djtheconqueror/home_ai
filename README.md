# Applied AI Portfolio: Biosecurity, Human Performance, and Biotech Analytics

This repository is a growing applied AI portfolio focused on building machine learning prototypes across biotechnology, human performance, biosecurity, and decision intelligence.

The goal of these projects is to develop practical technical fluency in Python, data science, machine learning, and explainable analytics while building toward a long-term interest in AI-driven biological systems, defense health, operational readiness, and biotechnology innovation.

## Portfolio Theme

I am using this repository to explore how AI can help translate complex biological, behavioral, environmental, and process signals into clearer decisions.

Current focus areas include:

- Biotechnology and lab-process analytics
- Human performance and readiness modeling
- Biosecurity and biosignal risk classification
- Decision intelligence and lifestyle/environmental recommendation systems
- Explainable machine learning prototypes

## Projects

### 1. GeoLifestyle AI

**Focus:** Decision intelligence / city-lifestyle recommendation

GeoLifestyle AI is a prototype recommendation system that scores cities based on lifestyle and environmental preferences such as region, rent, climate, diversity, culture, job market, safety, and overall lifestyle fit.

**Purpose:**  
To explore how AI and data-driven scoring can support personal decision-making around geography, lifestyle, and long-term planning.

---

### 2. BioSentinel v1

**Focus:** Biosecurity / biosignal risk classification

BioSentinel is an early-stage machine learning prototype focused on biosignal risk classification and explainable threat scoring.

The current version uses synthetic biosignal data and evaluates features such as:

- persistence
- volatility
- detection confidence
- toxicity index
- anomaly score

**Outputs include:**

- threat probability
- risk level
- model decision
- AI-style explanation of key signal drivers

**Purpose:**  
To explore how machine learning can support biological signal interpretation, biosecurity analytics, and explainable risk classification.

---

### 3. Readiness AI v1

**Focus:** Human performance / fatigue-readiness prediction

Readiness AI is an early-stage machine learning prototype focused on human performance readiness, fatigue risk, and operational health analytics.

The model uses synthetic readiness data and evaluates variables such as:

- sleep hours
- stress score
- hydration score
- soreness score
- training load
- resting heart rate
- cognitive fatigue

**Outputs include:**

- readiness prediction: READY / CAUTION / RECOVER
- readiness score
- AI-style explanation of readiness drivers

**Purpose:**  
To explore how AI can help interpret human performance signals and support decision-making around fatigue, recovery, and operational readiness.

Future directions include adding geographic and environmental readiness indicators such as climate, altitude, air quality, commute burden, disease trends, training environment, and recovery access.

---

### 4. BioPure AI v1

**Focus:** Biotechnology process modeling / purification prediction

BioPure AI is an early-stage machine learning prototype focused on predicting purification outcomes from synthetic bioprocess conditions.

The model evaluates variables such as:

- pH
- temperature
- flow rate
- protein concentration
- initial purity
- impurity level
- runtime
- buffer type
- column type

**Outputs include:**

- predicted purification yield
- success probability
- quality tier: Low / Medium / High
- AI-style explanation of key process drivers

**Purpose:**  
To connect biotechnology, lab-process optimization, and explainable machine learning through a practical purification prediction prototype.

### 5. Operational Biosecurity & Readiness AI v1

**Focus:** Biosecurity, human readiness, environmental risk, and operational decision support

This capstone project combines human readiness indicators, environmental/geographic context, and biosignal risk features to estimate operational biosecurity and readiness status.

The model evaluates signals such as sleep, stress, hydration, training load, cognitive fatigue, temperature, humidity, air quality, population density, local disease trend, biosignal anomaly, detection confidence, and signal persistence.

**Outputs include:**

- operational status: READY / MONITOR / MITIGATE / RECOVER
- overall risk score
- readiness risk
- environmental risk
- biosignal risk
- recommendation
- AI-style explanation of key risk drivers

**Purpose:**  
To connect prior applied AI projects into a broader decision-support prototype for biosecurity, human performance, and operational readiness analytics.
